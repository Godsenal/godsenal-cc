# comment-guide — 댓글·답글·유입 댓글

Read this before writing any comment. Every rule in [content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md) still applies (references first, no sources in public copy, no made-up numbers or experiences, official policy read before anything is automated, published only when the logged-out view shows it). A Threads reply's voice follows [threads-voice](../threads-voice/SKILL.md), an X quote post's voice and length follow [x-voice](../x-voice/SKILL.md); this skill holds where, how often, with which link, and when to stop. New feedback goes through content-common's feedback intake and lands here. Run-specific facts (which blog post has a wrong date, which channel to try next) stay in the caller's work folder (e.g. `$HOME/LTH/content-boost/GUIDE.md`), not here.

## 출력 언어

사용자에게 보이는 텍스트(진행 보고, 리포트)와 댓글 산출물(답글, 유입 댓글, 지식iN 답변, 인용 글)은 **한국어**로 쓴다. 아래 따옴표 안 한국어는 사용자 지시나 레퍼런스 원문이라 고치지 않는다. 코드·경로·명령어는 대상 레포의 관례를 따른다. 사용자가 다른 언어로 요청하면 그 언어를 따른다.

## Every comment

- **해요체, written like a person.** First read the comments and answers already on that post or video and follow their context and tone (user rule at the top of content-boost's GUIDE). One or two spoken sentences that react to one part of the post and add one thought; no stiff summary.
- **No promo phrasing**: "짧게 정리한 영상:", "정리한 글:", "쇼츠로 정리해뒀어요", and any "정리해 둔/정리한" (2026-10-29 11시: "표로 정리해 둔 글이에요" was caught and changed to "이 글 표에 나와 있어요"). Link the way people normally drop a link.
- **No source wording, no DM bait, no made-up experience** — no "출처", "○○ 발표에 따르면", "댓글 남기면 보내드려요", "써 보니" without a real case ([content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md)).
- **Nothing you couldn't confirm.** A sentence not confirmed on an official page is cut before posting (2026-10-28 12시, "빨래방 건조기가 열이 더 세다"). A sentence introducing a linked post says only what that post actually contains (2026-10-29 17시, "그림이랑 같이" cut). If the linked post's facts are out of date, don't mention that part.
- **Never the same target twice, never a reused sentence, never the same structure every time** — a comment that restates the post's numbers at length and ends "~할까요?" reads as a template (2026-10-28, observed on @pluskr and @apt_lap). Check the ledger before picking a target.
- **Check the logged-in account before typing** (YouTube @moneyalim, X @wolgeupnal, Instagram/Threads @haedollar; never a personal account), and log every comment (platform, account, target URL, permalink, time) in the work folder's ledger.

## (a) Replies to comments on our posts — Threads, X, Instagram

2026-10-29 (user decision): answer comments on our own posts. Measured: 11 of 17 sampled Threads accounts replied on their own posts (aicoffeechat, 19 replies on one post); on X, 17 of 20 money accounts did, on 101 of 140 posts. Instagram wasn't measured; the same @haedollar account follows the same rule.

- Answer a question briefly, react to thanks, in the voice of the post (threads-voice / x-voice; Instagram 해요체).
- A fact in a reply is checked like one in a post; if you can't confirm it, don't state it.
- No link or DM offer in a reply. If a commenter mistook a post for a DM-giveaway, say plainly that nothing is sent (예안 had to: "댓글달아쥬셔도 따로 가는 자료는 없습니당", [DasMiJkmOiZ](https://www.instagram.com/p/DasMiJkmOiZ/)).

## (b) Outreach comments on other people's posts

### Links and limits

| Platform (account) | Link | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube (@moneyalim) | Big channel (news/broadcaster — SBS, YTN, KBS, MBC, JTBC, 연합뉴스, 채널A, MBN, TV조선 … — or 50만+ subscribers): **no link**, content only. Smaller channel: **one** link, our matching short first, a Naver blog post only if no short fits; never a TikTok link | Same channel: one link comment a day |
| 지식iN | One matching link (short or blog post) | — |
| X (@wolgeupnal) | **No link.** Quote post + one line of opinion; never a link reply on someone else's post | — |
| Threads, Instagram (@haedollar) | **No link** (stage 1) | Threads 5/day, Instagram 3/day, 20+ min apart, alternate the two |

- **At most 2 link comments per round.** The round cap is 3 comments (2026-10-28 15:54), and since X dropped links (2026-10-29 14:00) only YouTube and 지식iN carry one. A Meta comment is added on top, without a link.
- Why the YouTube split (user decision 2026-10-28 16:46, kept on resume 23:41): 4 blog-link comments on big channels were missing from the logged-out list while small-channel ones stayed up. Blog-link comments kept getting hidden or deleted even on small channels; after the 23:41 resume the first real deletion was a blog link (fB2x-bzXWOc), so when no short fits, leave the slot empty rather than force a blog link.
- Why X changed (user decision 2026-10-29 14:00 "1. ㄱㄱ"): 18 of 32 @wolgeupnal posts were link replies and looked like spam; the money accounts that do well quote with one line and no link. Log the quote post's own permalink as "X 인용(@wolgeupnal)".
- No likes, no mass follows. Follow-backs follow content-common's follow-back rule.

### Picking targets

- **YouTube**: Korean video with Korean comments, uploaded within 2 weeks, that one of our shorts actually matches. A channel that only localized its titles (video and comments in English) is not a target.
- **지식iN**: a real person's question from the last 2–3 days, not yet chosen, with at most one answer. Answer the question directly, then add the official facts (who, period, where to check) — that shape got chosen (495721729, 495737416). Correcting a wrong existing answer with an official source also helps. Questions auto-posted by "지식iN서비스트렌드" rarely get chosen.
- **X**: an active Korean post from the last 24 hours that one of our topics fits; quote it with one line that answers or adds to its point.
- **Threads / Instagram**: a Korean AI or economy explainer post from the last 24 hours with 5 or fewer comments. Skip stock-pick spam, "댓글에 ○○ 남기면" giveaway posts (every comment is the same word), impersonation accounts (yoshisam_ri.ch), and tiny same-topic accounts unless you have a fact to add.

### Watching and stopping

- **Success = found logged out.** Find a comment with our author and text in the logged-out public list, record its ID, and check again next round: shorts-link comments seen right after posting were gone an hour later (CEiCxwV4x_Y, nCI4BypK8hE). Not found → `미확인` / 보류 추정, not counted. Never report someone else's comment as ours.
- **Hidden vs deleted** (YouTube): open the channel's comment history (`youtube.com/feed/history/comment_history`) — listed there but not public = hidden, gone = deleted. Hidden is reported, not a stop (2026-10-28 15:48 "그냥 계속하셈").
- **Stop YouTube at 2 real deletions** among comments posted since the last resume (counting restarted 2026-10-28 23:41), and report it. One deletion left → no blog links at all.
- **Threads / Instagram**: 2 hidden or deleted → stop that platform. Any suspension, "사람임을 확인하세요", or activity-limit screen, once → press nothing and stop **both** Instagram and Threads (same Meta account), and report it. Check for those screens before every Meta comment.
- A stop is lifted only by the user. Report causes as `가설:` and change these rules only on confirmed facts ([content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md)).

Platform mechanics that broke runs (editors, logged-out checks, search pacing) are in [references/operations.md](references/operations.md).
