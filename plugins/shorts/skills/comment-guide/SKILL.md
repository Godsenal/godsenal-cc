# comment-guide — 댓글·답글·유입 댓글

Read this before writing any comment. Every rule in [content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md) still applies (references first, no sources in public copy, no made-up numbers or experiences, official policy read before anything is automated, published only when the logged-out view shows it). A Threads reply's voice follows [threads-voice](../threads-voice/SKILL.md), an X quote post's voice and length follow [x-voice](../x-voice/SKILL.md); this skill holds where, how often, with which link, and when to stop. New feedback goes through content-common's feedback intake and lands here. Run-specific facts (which blog post has a wrong date, which channel to try next) stay in the caller's work folder (e.g. `$HOME/LTH/content-boost/GUIDE.md`), not here.

## 출력 언어

사용자에게 보이는 텍스트(진행 보고, 리포트)와 댓글 산출물(답글, 유입 댓글, 지식iN 답변, 인용 글)은 **한국어**로 쓴다. 아래 따옴표 안 한국어는 사용자 지시나 레퍼런스 원문이라 고치지 않는다. 코드·경로·명령어는 대상 레포의 관례를 따른다. 사용자가 다른 언어로 요청하면 그 언어를 따른다.

## Every comment

- **해요체, written like a person** (X: the [X section](#x--replies-and-comments) below sets length and tone). First read the comments and answers already on that post or video and follow their context and tone (user rule at the top of content-boost's GUIDE). One or two spoken sentences that react to one part of the post and add one thought; no stiff summary.
- **No promo phrasing**: "짧게 정리한 영상:", "정리한 글:", "쇼츠로 정리해뒀어요", and any "정리해 둔/정리한" (2026-10-29 11시: "표로 정리해 둔 글이에요" was caught and changed to "이 글 표에 나와 있어요"). Link the way people normally drop a link.
- **No source wording, no DM bait, no made-up experience** — no "출처", "○○ 발표에 따르면", "댓글 남기면 보내드려요", "써 보니" without a real case ([content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md)). Never a first-person story the user didn't live ("들고 가서 못 받았어요", caught 2026-10-29); say what the short or blog post shows instead ("영상에 준비물 나와요").
- **Nothing you couldn't confirm.** A sentence not confirmed on an official page is cut before posting (2026-10-28 12시, "빨래방 건조기가 열이 더 세다"). A sentence introducing a linked post says only what that post actually contains (2026-10-29 17시, "그림이랑 같이" cut). If the linked post's facts are out of date, don't mention that part.
- **Never the same target twice, never a reused sentence, never the same structure every time** — a comment that restates the post's numbers at length and ends "~할까요?" reads as a template (2026-10-28, observed on @pluskr and @apt_lap). Check the ledger before picking a target.
- **Check the logged-in account before typing** (YouTube @moneyalim, X @wolgeupnal, Instagram/Threads @haedollar; never a personal account), and log every comment (platform, account, target URL, permalink, time) in the work folder's ledger.

## (a) Replies to comments on our posts — Threads, X, Instagram

2026-10-29 (user decision): answer comments on our own posts. Measured: 11 of 17 sampled Threads accounts replied on their own posts (aicoffeechat, 19 replies on one post); on X, 17 of 20 money accounts did, on 101 of 140 posts. Instagram wasn't measured; the same @haedollar account follows the same rule.

- Answer a question briefly, react to thanks, in the voice of the post (threads-voice; X: the [X section](#x--replies-and-comments); Instagram 해요체).
- A fact in a reply is checked like one in a post; if you can't confirm it, don't state it.
- No link or DM offer in a reply, except our own library link when a commenter on a reward post says the DM didn't arrive ("DM이 늦게 갈 수 있어요. 프로필 링크 자료실에도 같은 자료 있어요" in your own words, never the same sentence twice; reward posts are [instagram-card-news](../instagram-card-news/SKILL.md)'s). On a post without a reward, if a commenter mistook it for a DM-giveaway, say plainly that nothing is sent (예안 had to: "댓글달아쥬셔도 따로 가는 자료는 없습니당", [DasMiJkmOiZ](https://www.instagram.com/p/DasMiJkmOiZ/)).

### Picking targets

**Pre-filter with Jev first** (user decision 2026-11-01, "댓글 사전 필터 넣어"). Save the scraped candidates (post objects with `text`) as JSON and run `python3 <this skill>/scripts/jev_prefilter.py cands.json -o cands.jev.json` before reading them. It drops spam (`noul` ≥ 0.5) and posts below `--min-fit 1.0` on a 0–3 "끼어들 만함" score, and sorts the rest best first; read `kept`, top down, then apply the rules below as usual. On 120 real X posts it agreed with Haiku on spam 119/120 and kept 56 of Haiku's 59 good targets while cutting the list to 75. It was measured on X only: for 지식iN, YouTube, Threads, and Instagram use `--min-fit 0` (spam only) until disagreements there are reviewed. No `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` or a Jev error → it keeps everything (`"jev": "skipped: …"`) and you read the whole list as before; put that reason in the run's notes.

- **YouTube**: Korean video with Korean comments, uploaded within 2 weeks, that one of our shorts actually matches. A channel that only localized its titles (video and comments in English) is not a target.
- **지식iN**: a real person's question from the last 2–3 days, not yet chosen, with at most one answer. Answer the question directly, then add the official facts (who, period, where to check) — that shape got chosen (495721729, 495737416). Correcting a wrong existing answer with an official source also helps. Skip the law category (법률/법학) and questions that already have an expert answer (변호사 등 전문가): answer registration is blocked there ("답변등록 불가", 2026-10-29). Prefer general categories; avoid 지식iN서비스트렌드-type categories and questions it auto-posted — they rarely get chosen.
- **X**: an active Korean post from the last 24 hours that one of our topics fits; quote it with one line that answers or adds to its point.
- **Threads / Instagram**: a Korean AI or economy explainer post from the last 24 hours with 5 or fewer comments. Skip stock-pick spam, "댓글에 ○○ 남기면" giveaway posts (every comment is the same word), impersonation accounts (yoshisam_ri.ch), and tiny same-topic accounts unless you have a fact to add.

### Watching and stopping

- **Success = found logged out.** Find a comment with our author and text in the logged-out public list, record its ID, and check again next round: shorts-link comments seen right after posting were gone an hour later (CEiCxwV4x_Y, nCI4BypK8hE). Not found → `미확인` / 보류 추정, not counted. Never report someone else's comment as ours.
- **Hidden vs deleted** (YouTube): open the channel's comment history (`youtube.com/feed/history/comment_history`) — listed there but not public = hidden, gone = deleted. Hidden is reported, not a stop (2026-10-28 15:48 "그냥 계속하셈").
- **Stop YouTube at 2 real deletions** among comments posted since the last resume (counting restarted 2026-10-28 23:41), and report it. One deletion left → no blog links at all.
- **Threads / Instagram**: 2 hidden or deleted → stop that platform. Any suspension, "사람임을 확인하세요", or activity-limit screen, once → press nothing and stop **both** Instagram and Threads (same Meta account), and report it. Check for those screens before every Meta comment.
- A stop is lifted only by the user. Report causes as `가설:` and change these rules only on confirmed facts ([content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md)).

## X — replies and comments

2026-10-29 18:16 the user: "댓글 남기는 것도 X 말투가 아님". Our 28 comments on other people's X posts had a median of 110자, none at 30자 or less, 20 with a link, and all 28 were "공감 한마디 + 사실 보충" — the shape automated accounts (afterwork2604, buup_data, goodm7203) leave, at ♥0–2. Measured the same day on 45 top Korean money/work posts: 314 author replies on their own posts (26 accounts) and 607 comments by others; the morning's 747 author replies and 1,350 comments matched. Data: `$HOME/.gbase/tasks/xvoice-1029/report.md`.

**Replies to comments on our posts**
- **One line of about 10자, never over 30자.** Author replies: median 10자, 95% at 30자 or less, 0 links in 314.
- **Follow the commenter's tone**: 존댓말 → 해요 (74), 반말 → 반말 or a short noun ending ("까먹음 ㅠㅠ"). Only 3 replied 반말 to 존댓말; don't.
- **Default is agreeing, empathy, or a joke** (82% new, 86% morning; information 5%). Give information only when asked, in one sentence.
- `ㅋㅋ`, `ㅠㅠ`, `!!` are normal (ㅋ 13–22%, ㅠ 8–10%). No "좋은 질문이에요" opener, no fixed sentence — donbaek2 pasted "도움이 되었다면 다행입니다" 12 times. Nothing more to add goes into a new root, not a longer reply.

Real examples: ppuujaa_m [link](https://x.com/ppuujaa_m/status/2107683283442270717) — to "맞다 이거 미리 한도 늘려놔야 해 ㅠㅠ": "늘려야지 했는데 까먹음 ㅠㅠㅠㅠ" · qq3540 [link](https://x.com/qq3540/status/2114853571888136488) — to "기본소득은 어디서 받죠??": "온라인 잡아바 어플라이에서 신청하심 됩니다!" · Buldakguy [link](https://x.com/Buldakguy/status/2110995605036404972) — to "중간에 깰 수 있어요 님아": "중간에 깰 거면 왜 함"

**On other people's posts**
- **Quote post first** (voice and length in [x-voice](../x-voice/SKILL.md)). A reply only where a quote doesn't fit: one line of 30자 or less, no link. Two different limits: a reply is ≤30자 (this skill), a quote post is 25–60자 (x-voice) (others' comments: median 13자, 83% at 30자 or less, 0 links in 607).
- One fact plus one feeling, not a summary: taehyun901 "t.m.i. 로 결제일 에서 +1일 후에 즉시결제 도 가능합니다" (♥5), sm749 "4번 의료급여 대상자 한정 됩니다 왜 이럼!", bansss_ "12퍼 이상은 아뭇따 / 기본은 고민좀 해야됨" (♥8).
- **Never "칭찬 + 보충 2문장 (+ 링크)"** and no source phrasing ("공단 안내상", "FAQ에도", "설명서 기준"). Rewritten from ours: "좋은 팁이에요. 올해부터 갱신 기간이 생일 전후 6개월로 바뀐 것도 같이 알아두면 좋아요. / 기간·준비물 정리한 글: (링크)" → "올해부터 생일 앞뒤 6개월로 바뀐 거 은근 모르더라구요"; "…서민금융통합지원센터(1397로 예약)로 가야 해서 … (링크)" → "2천 넘으면 온라인 안 되고 지점 가야 돼요 ㅠ".

Count before posting (same counter as x-voice's self-check): over 30자 for a reply, a link, source wording, or a first-person experience the user didn't have → rewrite. Report `voice-check: x 답글 1줄 14자 | 해요→해요 | 봇 신호 0`.
