# x-voice — X 글·인용 말투와 길이

Read this before writing anything for X. Every rule in [content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md) still applies (references first, no source credits, no made-up numbers or experiences, root holds the conclusion), and so does [hook-templates](../hook-templates/SKILL.md) for the first line, including its bans ("현직 OO"). Threads posts follow [threads-voice](../threads-voice/SKILL.md) instead. New feedback goes through content-common's feedback intake and lands here.

## 출력 언어

사용자에게 보이는 텍스트(진행 보고, 리포트)와 X 산출물(글, 인용 글, 이어쓰기)은 **한국어**로 쓴다. 아래 따옴표 안 한국어는 레퍼런스 원문이나 사용자 피드백 원문이라 고치지 않는다. 코드·경로·명령어는 대상 레포의 관례를 따른다. 사용자가 다른 언어로 요청하면 그 언어를 따른다.

## Why this skill exists

2026-10-29 18:16 the user: "말투도 X처럼 써야지. 댓글 남기는 것도 X 말투가 아님". The 해요체 ending wasn't the problem; the sentences were. Our 10 roots were 음슴체 in 8, and had 0 reader address, 0 reaction lines, 0 `ㅋ`/`ㅠ`, 0 question marks — they read as notices. Same topic, same week: our ⑤ 국민취업지원제도 root ("…국민취업지원제도부터 보는 게 순서") got 6 views and ♥0; qq3540's ("백수한테 주는 제도라고 함. / ㅅㅂ 아침밥 지원은 뭐냐 밥이야 알아서 먹겠지 / 1. 국민취업지원제도 - 월 60만원 …", [link](https://x.com/qq3540/status/2114893556498530315)) got ♥6,374. The difference was one human line, not the amount of information.

Measurements: 45 roots (38 money/life information, 7 work/money stories) from 36 accounts found with 15 searches (`lang:ko min_faves:300 since:2026-09-01`), each opened with its thread and replies; 219 quote posts on 18 of them; cross-checked against the morning's 140 posts of 20 money accounts. "Top" below means the upper half by ♥ within the sample. Raw data and tables: `$HOME/.gbase/tasks/xvoice-1029/report.md`.

## Root posts

- **First line talks to the reader.** "님들 ~아시나요", "~연장됐대", "~인 사람?", "오잉 너무 쉬워요". No notice-style opener: "~부터 보는 게 순서", "~해당", "~모음 (공고 원문 다 확인함)".
- **Information posts: spoken 해요체.** 해요/합쇼 was the most common ending among the top money posts (11 of 31 across both samples), but not a majority, so the ending alone doesn't make it sound like X. Write the 해요 people say — "~더라고요", "~거 아시나요", "~돼요 진짜", "~붙고요". **No 음슴체 information roots** (bottom 7 vs top 4 this sample; the morning's top 12 had 1). A one-to-three-line news item may be hearsay 반말 "~래/~대": all three top 반말 posts were short news of 76자 or less (kimtom_Official 33자 "06, 07년생 트친드라. / 문화향유 지원금 신청해. / 15~20만원 나온대", ♥8,575). Still no 반말 orders like "~셈", "~둬" (morning: 4 of 140, none in the top quartile).
- **Most roots have one reaction or attitude line, not every one.** Leave it out now and then (about 1 in 4–5 roots) when the fact itself carries the post, so the reaction line doesn't turn into a fixed pattern (user, 2026-10-29: "감상 줄만 유연하게"). "하 제발 중요한거인데", "아침밥 지원은 좀 웃기긴 함", "이건 진짜 챙길 만해요". Opinion and reaction only; no experience, amount, or count the user didn't have. Honest takes: posts with an opinion line beat the same account's other posts in 9 accounts and trailed in 3 (morning).
- **Reaction posts** (반말, 음슴체, `ㅋㅋ`, `..` fine) only about money or work, and only real: the user's own reaction or a feeling added to a fact in real news. Everyday-life monologue mostly underperformed (morning: 61 of 80 below the account median).

## Length and format

- **Information posts: body about 130자** (top half median 131자, bottom half 220자), first line about 20자 (top 17자). Figures as a short list ("1. 이름 - 금액"); everything past the numbers and the deadline is cut.
- **Reaction and opinion posts: about 50자** (morning top-quartile median 52자; 17 of 36 posts under 50자 were top quartile, 4 of 25 over 300자).
- **Emoji 0–1**; no ✦·1️⃣·✔️ decorated lists (top half 2 posts with emoji, bottom half 6, e.g. donbaek2 at 1,128자).
- **Thread: 0–1 continuation** that extends the body or adds a short aside. Never one that holds only a source or a link.

## Quote posts — quote + one line of opinion

- **One or two lines, 25–60자.** All 219: median 24자, 64% one line; ♥20+ ones median 66자. No link, source, hashtag, or mention (1 link in 219).
- **반말, 음슴, or noun-style monologue.** Only 3 of the 42 quotes with ♥20+ were 해요 (31 of 219 overall). Mix in "~맞아요" only when correcting the original.
- **Say where you stand**: correct or add a condition ("~만 연장되는 거고 ~는 끝남"), push back ("그렇게 취급하면 안됨"), or say what you really think ("그사이에 돈다써버릴거같애"). A number or condition helps (22 of the 42 with ♥20+).
- **No summary or praise of the original** ("정리 깔끔하네요", "좋은 정보네요", "~라는 점이 핵심이네요") — that is the shape automated accounts (afterwork2604, buup_data) leave, at ♥0–2. Long 해요 information quotes got almost nothing (working_00_home "전세사기 피해를 예방하기 위해 세입자가 실천해야 할 8가지 …", ♥8).
- First-person experience only if it happened; no job framing like "은행원입니다" (we aren't one).

## Replies, comments, and engaging

- **Replies to comments on our posts and comments on other people's posts follow [comment-guide](../comment-guide/SKILL.md)'s X section** (voice, length, links, when to quote instead, limits, stop rules).
- **No source wording anywhere**: no source reply, no "(2026.10.27 확인)", no "출처", no "(공고 원문 다 확인함)", "바뀌면 이 타래 고침", "출처는 답글에". None of the 45 top posts or their threads had one. Sources stay in the work report (content-common).
- No DM bait, no band or Telegram links, no "맞팔드려요" in copy. Following back is content-common's follow-back rule (follow back by default, skip impersonation/spam/bots, ≤5 per round, logged), not a post.

## Bot signals — if any of these is in the draft, rewrite it

1. Notice-style first line ("~보는 게 순서", "~해당", "~모음 (원문 확인함)"), or an 음슴체 information root.
2. Several roots in a row with no human line — no reader address, no take, no `ㅋ`/`ㅠ`, no `?` (one plain root now and then is fine). Also the same reaction line pattern repeated across posts.
3. A reaction post over ~100자, an information post well past 130자, or decorated emoji lists.
4. A quote that only summarizes or praises the original, or a 해요 information quote.
5. Any link in a quote, source wording, check date, or source-only continuation.
6. The same sentence frame or ending as the previous post.
7. An experience the user didn't have, or a job framing.

## Self-check before handing off

Count, don't estimate. Save the post to a file and run:

```bash
python3 -c 'import re,sys;t=open(sys.argv[1]).read().strip();L=[l for l in t.splitlines() if l.strip()];print(len(L),"줄",len("".join(L)),"자 | 첫줄",len(L[0]),"자 | 숫자후보",re.findall(r"\d+(?:[.,]\d+)*",t),"| 링크",bool(re.search(r"https?://|\.com|\.kr",t)),"| 감정표지",bool(re.search(r"ㅋ|ㅠ|ㅜ|ㄷㄷ|!|\?|진짜|제발",t)),"| 출처말",bool(re.search(r"출처|확인\)|안내상|FAQ",t)))' post.txt
```

Root: about 130자, 감정표지 True for most roots (an occasional False is fine). Quote: 60자 or less. Both: 링크 and 출처말 False. Walk the bot-signal list, then put one line in the work report, for example `voice-check: x 인용 1줄 38자 | 반말 정정 | 봇 신호 0`.
