## Platform notes

From the post's author: on X, lighter hooks with a 'ㅋㅋ' feel work better than these templates; on Instagram and Threads the templates work as written. Adapt a template to the topic rather than pasting the form.

- **Naver blog**: the search keyword goes at the front of the title, so a template only fills the end of the title and the first paragraph.
- **Shorts / Reels / TikTok**: the template is the on-screen headline that the 0.5–1s cover frame shows ([shorts-common](../shorts-common/SKILL.md) length rule), and may be the caption's hook line.
- **Card news**: the cover or first card; 8, 14, 22 fit carousels.
- **Threads**: a template is only a starting point. The first line stays within [threads-voice](../threads-voice/SKILL.md)'s caps (20자 or less) and voice, so trim the template into how a person would say it.

## 머니알림 rules (user-confirmed, 2026-10-28)

- **Good fits**: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 10, 14, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23 — money and everyday-life information that viewers save.
- **Banned: 6 and 18.** 6 "현직 OO" claims a job the channel operator doesn't hold (false representation). 18 "상위 1%만 아는 주식정보"-style hooks read as investment solicitation and exaggeration and risk platform-policy violations. The same goes for any hook that impersonates or inflates this way, template or not (2026-10-29).
- **Experience hooks (1, 3, 9, 17, 24, 25, or any "써보니 …") only on a real case** — the user's, or one from an opened source noted in the report, never credited in the copy ([content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md)) — and never with a number that didn't happen (days, kg, 만원, 1년). A number from neither the case's source nor the user is a `가정:` line ([content-common](../content-common/SKILL.md)) and stays out of the hook.
- **This is a reference list, not the rule.** Top references actually opened for the piece and the platform's official policy decide which hook to use; the bans above hold either way. Don't reuse the same template in consecutive posts; note the template number used in the report so the next post can check.
