# Short-form trends — researched 2026-10-26

Dated snapshot; platform rules move. Re-check the official pages before relying on a number, and re-research when this file is more than ~6 months old. Tags: **[official]** platform help/blog/terms, **[press]** official statement seen only via news, **[3P]** third-party study, **[anecdotal]** rule of thumb with no traceable data.

User feedback in `shorts-common` wins over anything here — these are background, not specs.

## Hook (first 1–2 seconds)
- [official] Shorts in the feed have no thumbnail step: the video starts and the viewer stays or swipes; a Short that underperforms others in the feed "won't go much beyond those initial views". — https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-march-4-2024/ (2024-04)
- [official] YouTube Studio reports "viewed vs. swiped away" (some surfaces: "Stayed to watch") per Short — the metric to read for hook quality. — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217
- [anecdotal] "70–80% viewed" is a guide-writer benchmark, not a YouTube number; compare against the channel's own Shorts instead.
- Not found: any platform-published figure for drop-off in seconds 1–3.

## Cut frequency / pacing
- [3P] Controlled experiment (Dost & Huang 2026, n=242, one 20s clip): overlapping cuts raised completion and rewatch, but the gain shrank as cuts got more frequent; faster pacing overall lowered sustained engagement. More cuts is not automatically better. — https://archives.marketing-trends-congress.com/2026/pages/PDF/paper_professor_DOST_HUANG.pdf
- [anecdotal] "A cut every 1.5–2s" has no traceable source.

## Captions
- [3P] Meta ad tests: captioned video ads ≈ +12% view time; Verizon Media/Publicis: up to 69% watch sound-off in public. The common "85% watch muted" traces to 2016 Facebook self-reports. — https://www.opus.pro/blog/youtube-shorts-caption-subtitle-best-practices
- Burned-in subtitles stay the default for clip formats (foreign dialogue needs them anyway).

## Loopability / views
- [press] Since 2025-03-31 a Shorts view counts every start or replay; YPP still uses "engaged views". — https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/
- [official, snippet] TikTok qualified views (Creator Rewards) need ≥5s watched and count repeat views from one account once.
- Not found: any official statement that loops boost ranking. A clean last frame (no black frame) still helps replays read as continuous.

## Length and monetization
- [official, snippet] TikTok Creator Rewards: original, **over 1 minute**, ≥1,000 For You views; account ≥10k followers, ≥100k views/30d, 19+ in Korea, personal account. — https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program (page shows updated 2026-09-10) — this is why the common skill pins 61–65s.
- [press] YouTube Shorts up to 3 min (uploads from 2024-10-15). YPP: 1k subs + 4k watch hours/12mo or 10M valid Shorts views/90d. — https://petapixel.com/2024/10/04/youtube-shorts-expands-video-length-to-three-minutes/
- [press] Instagram Reels up to 3 min (2025-01). Whether only ≤90s Reels are recommended to non-followers is unconfirmed after that change. — https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21
- [press] Reels top signals (Mosseri, 2025-01): watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach.

## Reused / clipped content — matters for foreign-clip formats
- [official] YouTube "reused content": repurposing "without adding significant original commentary, substantive modifications, or educational or entertainment value" isn't monetizable. Allowed: commentary, reactions with commentary, edits that add a storyline or voiceover. Not allowed: minimal edits, low-narrative compilations, compilations from other social platforms. 2025-07-15: "repetitious" renamed "inauthentic content" (templated, mass-produced, interchangeable videos). — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
  - Implication: narration bridges, Korean subtitles, and a story structure are what keep a clip short (and TOP3 compilations especially) on the right side of this line.
- [3P reporting] TikTok: unoriginal reuploads and watermarked posts are ineligible for For You; repeat offenders lose FYF at account level. — https://www.engadget.com/tiktok-is-trying-to-clean-up-its-for-you-recommendations-210057825.html
- [official] Instagram down-ranks reposted, low-res, or watermarked Reels; [press] accounts reposting others' content 10+ times in 30 days lose recommendations (2024-04), extended to photos/carousels 2026-04. — https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained, https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/30/instagram-is-updating-its-ranking-systems-to-surface-more-content-from-smaller-original-creators

## AI labeling
- [official] YouTube: disclose realistic altered/synthetic content via Studio's "altered content" setting; AI used only for scripts/captions is exempt. — https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
- [official] TikTok: label realistic AI content; auto-labels on C2PA metadata; labeled content isn't demoted for that alone. — https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content
- [official] Meta: photorealistic video / realistic audio must be disclosed ("AI info" label). — https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/
